ChangeLink · kiosk demo

Leftover foreign notes → USDC straight into your wallet　·　drag to rotate　·　scroll to zoom

What this is

Travellers feed in the foreign notes they can no longer spend; the machine verifies each one and sends the same value as USDC straight to their own wallet — no sign-up, no account, no custody.

interactive prototypedemo data

How to drive it

  1. Press Start and pick a currency
  2. Drag a note from the rack into the intake slot — each note is verified
  3. Counterfeit / unrecognised / over-cap notes are ejected on the spot
  4. Type your wallet address on the on-screen base58 keyboard
  5. Confirm the quote (rate locked for 30s) → USDC goes to your wallet; the done screen shows the Solana hash
